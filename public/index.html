Every claim traced back to the audio

Check what a lecture, podcast or talk actually says.

Paste a YouTube link. You get a short report of the recording where every sentence is checked against the transcript. Click a sentence to jump to the moment it comes from. Sentences the recording does not support are flagged, with the reason.

About a minute per hour of audio. Results are saved and shareable.
